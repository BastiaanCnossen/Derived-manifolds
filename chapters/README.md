# Chapter files

The fourteen numbered files follow the order of `program.tex`. In particular,
Chapter 6 develops derived linear algebra and cotangent complexes, Chapter 7 treats
quasi-smooth local geometry, and Chapter 8 covers derived bordism.

The chapters are textbook-style notes for the corresponding talks. Their final
sections collect further examples, proofs, or reading beyond the core talk.
Chapter 3 places its detailed transverse inverse-image globalization after
the main geometric conclusions. The front matter describes the main reading
route, and Chapter 11 gives the three-step map of the representability proof.
Historical dossiers and timed outlines in `planning/` record earlier versions;
the current program determines the assignment and emphasis.
